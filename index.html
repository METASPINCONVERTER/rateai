How the score works

A tool's score is the mean of every rating it has received, shown to one decimal place with the rating count beside it. The count is never hidden, because a 5.0 from two people and a 4.6 from twelve thousand are not the same claim.

The leaderboard ranks by a weighted average that pulls low-sample tools toward the catalogue mean, so a single five-star rating cannot outrank years of accumulated opinion. Tools with no ratings are listed and searchable, but never ranked.
