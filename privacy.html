Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

PC Remote is designed around local control between your Android device and Windows PC. Privacy and data minimization are core principles of our architecture.

Local Connection & Data Handling

All remote control commands—including mouse movements, keystrokes, and media actions—are transmitted directly over your local Wi-Fi network or USB connection.

  • No cloud account is required to use PC Remote.
  • Input signals are not relayed through cloud servers.
  • Pairing PINs and session tokens are stored locally on your devices.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PC Remote security, contact us at support@pcremote.app.
